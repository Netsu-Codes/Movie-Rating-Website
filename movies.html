    Cheng Xiaoshi

    Cheng Xiaoshi (Chinese: 程小时 Chéng Xiǎoshí) is the main protagonist of the Link Click donghua series and a member of the Time Photo Studio. He has the ability to enter the world within a photograph at the moment it was taken, possessing the photographer and controlling their actions. Alongside Lu Guang, he can share his consciousness with him to investigate events within a photo in order to fulfill their clients wishes, and even alter the events of the past.

    Lu Guang

    Lu Guang (Chinese: 陆光 Lù Guāng) is the deuteragonist of the Link Click donghua series and a member of the Time Photo Studio. He has the ability to see everything that happens within a photograph for up to 12 hours after it was taken. Alongside Cheng Xiaoshi, he can share his consciousness and direct what he can and cannot do while inside a photo. He has traveled back in time, manipulating the past in an attempt to alter Cheng Xiaoshi's death node and prevent his death.

    Qiao Ling

    Qiao Ling (Chinese: 乔苓 Qiáo Líng) is the tritagonist of the Link Click donghua series. She is the landlady of the Time Photo Studio, a childhood friend of Cheng Xiaoshi, and a close friend of Lu Guang, whom she met at university. She watches over the two while attracting clients to the studio as they work to pay off their debt to her. She inherited Li Tianxi's ability after her death and can observe the current actions of the person in a photo.

    Xia Fie

    Xia Fei (Chinese: 夏斐 Xià Fěi) is a character from the Link Click donghua series. He was a college student in Bridon who became a part-time model and quickly became famous. He now works various odd jobs just to make ends meet.

    Vien

    Vein, full name Xiao Weiying (Chinese: 萧未影 Xiāo Wèiyǐng) is a major antagonist from the Link Click donghua series. He is the current leader of the Bridon Chinatown Chamber of Commerce and works as a manager at a modeling agency on the side.

    Jay lee

    Jae Lee, also known as JAE (Chinese: 李孑 Lǐ jié), is a character from the Link Click donghua series. He is a police officer in the Bridon Crime Agency and came to Guidu to investigate a resurgence of superpower related crimes.

    Li Tianchen

    Li Tianchen (Chinese: 李天辰 Lǐ Tiānchén) is a major reoccurring antagonist from the Link Click donghua series and the twin brother of Li Tianxi. He originally worked with Qian Jin and was responsible for a series of serial murders, and is now a member of Liu Xiao's revived Saros Society.

    Li Tianxli

    Li Tianxi (Chinese: 李天希 Lǐ Tiānxī) is a character from the Link Click donghua series and the younger twin sister of Li Tianchen. She had the ability to observe the current actions of a person in a photo, which she later transferred to Qiao Ling upon her death.

    Lui Xiao

    Liu Xiao (Chinese: 刘枭 Liú Xiāo) is the overarching antagonist in the Link Click donghua series and the younger half-brother of Liu Min. He has dedicated his life since childhood to investigating the origins of superpowers and restoring the Saros Society.

    A Yuang

    A Yuan (Chinese: 阿元 Ā Yuán) is a character from the Link Click donghua series. He is a member of Liu Xiao's revived Saros Society and can control another person's actions by issuing them verbal commands.

    Wang Qing

    Wang Qing (Chinese: 王青 Wáng Qīng) is a character from the Link Click donghua series. She was a student at the Bahati Language School and was declared the only survivor after the school burned down. She is now a psychotherapist in Bridon and has the ability to induce a fake death in a target.
